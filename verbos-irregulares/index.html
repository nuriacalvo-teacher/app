Grammar Guide

How to answer

  • Write only the missing verb in the gap. The base form is in brackets at the end: (go).
  • Finished time (yesterday, last week, ago, in 2010) → past simple: I went ...
  • After have / has → past participle: I have gone ...
  • Capital letters don't matter. When the list gives two forms (learnt / learned), both are correct.
  • You need at least 90% (45 out of 50) to pass. Your progress is saved if you close the page.
