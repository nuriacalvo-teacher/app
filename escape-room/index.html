How to play

One screen, several teams. Project the vault on the board. Teams take turns: one player comes to the board and answers for the whole team.

Five chambers. Each chamber trains one skill — irregular verbs, present tenses, vocabulary, word order and habits. Every correct answer reveals one letter of the chamber keystone.

Keystones. When the questions are over, any team can type the keystone word to unlock the chamber (+15 points). The first letter of each keystone goes to the master code.

Hearts and heroes. A wrong answer costs the team hearts. Each team picks a hero class with one power per chamber: reveal options, freeze the clock, a second chance, extra time or a shield.

The Guardian. After the last chamber the Guardian appears: rapid questions from every chamber damage it. Beat it, type the master code and the vault opens.

Keyboard: 1–4 or A–D to answer · Enter to submit · Space to continue · H for a power.
